A few [outdoor projects](https://www.nar.realtor/research-and-statistics/research-reports/remodeling-impact-report-outdoor-features){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"} can really upgrade a house, sometimes enough to make it feel like a completely new home. Three stand out to me, and together they cover the backyard, the front yard, and the view from the street.

**1\. Refresh the landscaping, front or back.** If you’re thinking about [freshening up your house](https://blog.samlevyrealestate.com/3-simple-upgrades-to-improve-your-home-s-value.html){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, the yard is a natural place to begin, whether that means the backyard, the front yard, or both. Adding turf, putting in pavers, and similar updates can give the entire property a noticeably fresher look.

**2\. Add a Phoenix-ready outdoor kitchen.** An outdoor kitchen is [the more expensive project](https://blog.samlevyrealestate.com/remodeling-the-best-bang-for-your-buck.html){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"} on this list, though it suits life here well. Phoenix enjoys great weather most of the year, and adding a grill, a fridge, and a few similar pieces lets you enjoy far more of it from your backyard.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**3\. New garage door, new curb appeal.** The last project is purely about curb appeal, since a new garage door completely changes how a house feels from the street.
