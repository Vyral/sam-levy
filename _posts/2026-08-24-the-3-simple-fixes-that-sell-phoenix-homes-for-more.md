If you are thinking about selling your home, there are a couple of upgrades you can make to help improve the value you'll get for it. These are simple and easy to do, and none of them require a major remodel. A little effort in the right places goes a long way. Here are the three I always point sellers toward.

**First, update the countertops.** If you have old, outdated counters in the kitchen or bathrooms, swapping them for a bright white or light quartz, or even a granite counter, can change the entire feel of the home. Counters are one of the first things a buyer notices, so a fresh surface makes the whole space feel newer and more cared for.

**Second, add a fresh coat of paint.** Painting the house a nice neutral color makes the home feel clean and vibrant, and it's one of the most affordable updates you can make. You can take it a step further and paint the cabinets a neutral color too, which gives the kitchen a refreshed look without the cost of replacing anything.

{% include pullquote.html %}

**Third, declutter and depersonalize.** This one's obvious, but it matters. Before you list, clear out the extra clutter and take down the personal items. Not everybody wants to walk in and see a ton of family photos or a room full of toys. Depersonalizing helps a buyer picture themselves living there, and it makes a real difference once you get [professional photos done and the home ready for showings](https://blog.samlevyrealestate.com/how-to-impress-buyers-during-showings.html){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

So just to reiterate: paint, countertops, and depersonalizing. Those three things alone will help you get more money for your home, and none of them will break the bank.
